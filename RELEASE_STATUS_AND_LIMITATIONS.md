# Release Status and Limitations

## Technical status

The 1.0.0 package passes the executed source, preservation, build, syntax, local-resource, hosted-runtime, `file://` offline, search, mobile-overflow and automated accessibility checks documented in `QA_REPORTS/`.

## Scientific status

The content completed an automated and editorial scientific/safety risk screen, but it has **not** received a named independent line-by-line clinical peer review in this project. Do not describe the product as clinically validated, medically certified or approved by the organisations cited in the books.

## Commercial decision

The files include a licence, privacy notice, disclaimer, attribution, version history, manifests and QA evidence. Before sale, the rights holder should:

1. obtain legal review of the licence, disclaimer, consumer terms and asset rights for the intended market;
2. confirm the seller/contact identity and refund/support terms outside or alongside this package;
3. retain proof of rights for all supplied photographs, illustrations, fonts and manuscripts;
4. prioritise professional review of emergency, allergy, nutrient/dose, medicine, pregnancy and disease-management content;
5. test the final download and purchase flow on the actual storefront.

These are governance and professional-review limitations, not hidden technical failures. They are disclosed to prevent unsupported release claims.
