Privacy & Accessibility

No account required. No personal medical records or health measurements are collected or transmitted. Only reading preferences, chapter progress, bookmarks, and non-sensitive study notes are saved locally in your browser.

Privacy, Local Storage & Medical Safeguards

Zero External Data Collection: HERBULANCE Book 18 runs entirely in your browser. It sends zero analytics, zero tracking scripts, and zero reader input to external servers.

What Is Stored Locally (Browser localStorage):

  • Reading display preferences (light/dark reading mode and text size).
  • Completed chapters checklist and bookmarked chapters.
  • Non-sensitive study notes written by you.
  • Quiz review scores, flashcard mastery markers, and 30-day habit reflection checkboxes.
Important Health Privacy Rule: Please do not enter sensitive personal medical records, clinical diagnoses, exact blood pressure readings, lipid values, or prescription details into browser study notes. All measurement templates in the Worksheets section are educational formats designed to be printed or written offline and shared directly with your healthcare professional.
