# HERBULANCE Book 28 — Quick Start

**Title:** Ear Health, Hearing & Balance  
**Collection:** HERBULANCE — The Complete Life Toolkit  
**Volume:** 28  
**Edition:** Digital edition 1.0 (2026)  
**Language:** English

## Open the book

Open `index.html` in a current browser. The production edition is compiled as a single, self-contained HTML file: styles, interface and content are embedded. No login, subscription, paid API or internet connection is required. External source links open only when clicked.

## Five useful first steps

1. **Read the safety guide.** Open **Start here** from the navigation. Read the urgent-warning guide and medical disclaimer.
2. **Choose a route.** Use **Contents** or the left sidebar to select any of the 22 chapters across five parts.
3. **Use the reading tools.** Search with the top-bar search (Ctrl/⌘ + K). Save a chapter with **Bookmark**. Mark a chapter complete at the end.
4. **Visit the Learning Studio.** Explore the interactive ear and vestibular diagrams, compare illustrative audiograms, estimate noise dose, flip flashcards and practise urgency recognition with fictional scenarios.
5. **Use a worksheet.** Open **Print & plan** in the Learning Studio, choose one of 13 printable forms, write your notes and select **Print / PDF**.

## Reading features

- Search chapters, glossary terms, FAQs and pages locally.
- Bookmarks, reading completion and worksheet entries save in browser local storage on the current device.
- Dark and light reading modes are available from the top-right control.
- Print styles are provided for chapters and worksheets.
- The application is responsive for mobile, tablet and desktop.

## Important safety notice

This is general health education, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It has not received individual review or endorsement by a named physician, audiologist or ENT specialist.

Seek emergency medical help for sudden severe vertigo with facial drooping, weakness, speech difficulty, severe headache, confusion or inability to walk. Seek urgent assessment for sudden hearing loss, major ear trauma, a suspected button battery, signs of serious infection or rapidly worsening symptoms. Do not put objects, oils or unadvised products into the ear.

## Privacy and storage

No account or remote database is used. Reading progress, bookmarks and worksheet entries are stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser data, changing browsers or using a private window may remove them. The book does not include a symptom checker or diagnostic engine.

## Offline and print

- For a permanent local copy, use **About this edition → Save offline HTML** from the deployed single-file edition.
- Use **Print / PDF** on worksheets. Choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog where available.
- Third-party online sources require internet access and may change after publication.

## Support and limitations

Hearing and balance guidance varies by country and health system. The book cannot determine local service availability, device funding or individual clinical suitability. Consult a qualified local professional for personal decisions.
